About the Artist

Meet Hxx

Hello, I'm Hxx

I am a freelance illustrator based in China, dedicated to creating visual stories that speak to the heart. My work spans illustrated picture books, book cover designs, and experimental pop-up books that bring stories to life in three dimensions.

Drawing has always been my way of understanding the world. Every line carries an emotion, every color tells a story. I believe that the best illustrations are not just beautiful to look at — they make you feel something.

When I'm not illustrating, you'll find me exploring old bookstores, collecting vintage paper scraps, or wandering through nature in search of the next story waiting to be told.

— Hxx
